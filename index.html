Our goal

Be active every day and feel the difference

LINE helps teenagers (13 to 17) move at least 60 minutes a day for four weeks. You follow a simple plan, track your daily habits and learn how exercise helps your body and your mind. Four weeks is a good start for building a healthy routine.

For your body

  • Stronger muscles and bones
  • A healthier heart and lungs
  • More energy every day

For your mind

  • Less stress and worry
  • A better mood
  • Better focus at school
  • Better sleep at night

For your habits

  • Drink 8 glasses of water
  • Eat fruit and vegetables
  • Sleep 8 to 10 hours

Five motivation tips

  1. Start small and add a little each week.
  2. Find a friend to move with.
  3. Tick every day to keep your line going.
  4. Mix activities so it never gets boring.
  5. Rest days are part of the plan.

Weekly challenge: 7 on the line

Walk 8,000 steps every day for 7 days. Take a friend with you at least once. Tick all 7 days on the calendar to finish the week.

LINE is a school project about a healthy lifestyle. If you have a health problem, ask a doctor before you start a new exercise plan.
