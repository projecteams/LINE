Simple advice

10 tips for a healthy life

  • Eating

    Eat fruit or vegetables with every meal.

  • Eating

    Start the day with breakfast. Eggs, oats or yoghurt give you energy for school.

  • Eating

    Keep sweets and fizzy drinks for treats. Choose water most days.

  • Water

    Drink 6 to 8 glasses a day. Drink more when you train.

  • Sleep

    Sleep 8 to 10 hours. Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day.

  • Sleep

    Put your phone away one hour before bed.

  • Screen time

    Keep free-time screens to about 2 hours a day. When you study, stand up and move every 30 minutes.

  • Stress

    Try slow breathing, a walk outside, or talk to someone you trust.

  • Motivation

    Set small goals. "Run for 10 minutes" is easier than "get fit".

  • Motivation

    Exercise with friends and celebrate every finished week.
