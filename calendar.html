Your calendar

Green square: day done. Dashed square: rest day. Tap a square to open that week.

    Every workout

    Warm up, cool down, rest

    Warm-up (5 min)

    • March on the spot, 2 minutes
    • Arm circles and leg swings, 1 minute each
    • Jumping jacks, 1 minute

    Cool-down (5 min)

    • Slow walk, 2 minutes
    • Stretch legs and shoulders, 20 seconds each
    • Five deep breaths

    Rest days

    Thursday and Sunday in every plan. Your body gets stronger while it rests. In Free training you choose your own rest days.

    Weekly goals

    • Week 1: build the habit
    • Week 2: build stamina
    • Week 3: get stronger
    • Week 4: push yourself and celebrate

    LINE is a school project about a healthy lifestyle. If you have a health problem, ask a doctor before you start a new exercise plan.
