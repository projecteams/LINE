Good to know

Before you start

How much to move

Teenagers should move at least 60 minutes every day (WHO advice). Most of it should be cardio. Add muscle and bone exercises on at least 3 days a week.

Possible risks

  • Skipping the warm-up
  • Doing too much too soon
  • Not drinking enough water

Start slowly. Stop if something hurts.

Equipment

Comfortable trainers, a water bottle and a mat. A skipping rope, a ball and a backpack are optional. Many exercises need nothing at all.

LINE is a school project about a healthy lifestyle. If you have a health problem, ask a doctor before you start a new exercise plan.
